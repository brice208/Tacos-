Our Story

Made with Flavor, Served with Heart

At Taquería Machetes, every taco is a labor of love. Nestled in the heart of Kerrville on Sidney Baker St, we've been serving the Hill Country with authentic Mexican flavors that keep guests coming back again and again.

Our meats are seasoned to perfection — birria, barbacoa, bistec, and more — cooked just right, with tortillas so fresh and authentic you'd swear they came straight from a grandmother's comal.

From a quiet breakfast taco to a full birria feast, we've got your table covered seven days a week.
